01 What is D2C SEO?

It is SEO built around how D2C brands make money: collection pages, product pages and buying-intent content that bring in orders, not just traffic.

02 What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization means getting your brand cited and recommended in AI answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.

03 Do you work with Shopify stores?

Yes. Most of my D2C work is as a Shopify SEO freelancer, including technical fixes, collections, product schema and Merchant Center.

04 How long does D2C SEO take to show results?

Most brands see early movement in 2 to 3 months and meaningful revenue growth in 4 to 6 months, depending on the site's current state and competition.
