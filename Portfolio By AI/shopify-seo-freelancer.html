Key Takeaways

What I have learnt from growing D2C stores

Practical principles discovered while building organic revenue for active brands.

01

Collection pages bring the money, not blogs. On GGT Foods, most of the revenue growth came after fixing collections and long-tail product pages. Blogs came later.

02

AI-written blogs rarely bring traffic. Replacing them with fewer, well-researched topics and adding product CTAs worked better than publishing more.

03

Search Console title rewrites are the quickest win. On AllOfficials, rewriting collection titles from real query data lifted clicks without any new content.

04

Traffic without CRO leaks sales. Session recordings showed where shoppers dropped off. Fixing those pages made the same traffic convert better.

05

Gifting and seasonal pages need a head start. Build and index them well before the season so they rank when demand peaks.

06

AI answers follow mentions. Brands that get talked about on Reddit and in "best of" lists are the ones AI tools recommend.

Common Questions

Questions founders ask before hiring a Shopify SEO freelancer

Everything you need to know about scaling organic revenue on Shopify.

01 What does a Shopify SEO freelancer do?

A Shopify SEO freelancer improves how your store ranks on Google and AI search. The work covers collection and product page optimisation, technical fixes, content and links. The goal is more orders from organic search.

02 How is a D2C SEO expert different from a general SEO consultant?

A D2C SEO expert works on revenue pages first: collections, products and buying guides. A general consultant often starts with blogs and rankings. I report on organic revenue and orders, not just traffic.

03 How much does a Shopify SEO freelancer cost?

The cost depends on the number of products and collections, how competitive the category is and whether you need content and links. Book a call and I will share a quote after a free audit.

04 How long does Shopify SEO take to show results?

You usually see early movement in 2 to 3 months and meaningful revenue growth in 4 to 6 months. GGT Foods grew organic revenue by 195% in 6 months.

05 Should I hire a Shopify SEO freelancer or an agency?

Hire a freelancer if you want a senior person doing the work and a flexible contract. Hire an agency if you need many services under one roof and have the budget for it.

06 Do I need a Shopify SEO app?

Apps help with small tasks like bulk alt text or redirects. They cannot fix collection structure, keyword mapping or content. Too many apps also slow the store down.

07 Will you also handle GEO and AI search visibility?

Yes. Every plan includes an AI visibility audit and monthly tracking of how your brand appears in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI answers.

08 Do you work with brands in any country?

Yes. I work remotely with D2C brands across markets and time zones.
